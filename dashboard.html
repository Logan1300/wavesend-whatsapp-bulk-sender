      Help

      How WaveSend works and how to keep your number safe.

      Quick start

      1. Open WhatsApp Web in Chrome and scan the QR code once.
      2. In New campaign, upload your Excel/CSV, a Google Sheet link, or paste numbers.
      3. Write 2–3 variations using {{name}}. Keep the risk meter green.
      4. Add files if needed, send a test to your own number, then Start.
      5. A day later, open Reports & replies and click Check replies to see who read, replied, is interested or wants to stop – then send a follow-up to the right group.

      Something not working?

      Open WhatsApp Web, open any chat (e.g. your own number), then click Diagnose. It checks every part of WhatsApp Web that WaveSend uses and gives you a report you can copy and share for a quick fix. Nothing is sent and no messages are read.

      Troubleshooting

      • Paused – not logged in: scan the QR code in WhatsApp Web, then Resume.
      • “Use here” popup: WhatsApp is open in another window or the desktop app. Click “Use here”, then Resume.
      • Many “failed”: keep the WhatsApp tab visible (its own window works), reload it, then Resume.
      • After updating the extension: reload the WhatsApp Web tab.

      Why numbers get banned

      WhatsApp restricts numbers mainly when recipients block or report them, and when activity looks automated: identical messages, very fast sending, many chats with numbers that aren't on WhatsApp, and a sudden jump in volume on a new number. No tool can make bulk messaging risk-free; the Safety Shield handles the automated-looking parts, and you control who you message.

      • Message people who know you or asked to hear from you.
      • Warm up new numbers and keep the daily limit modest.
      • Personalise and vary every message; avoid links and salesy words in the first message.
      • Let people opt out and honour it (automatic in WaveSend).
      • Watch your health score in Safety Shield and follow its recommendations.
      • For very large marketing volumes, use the official WhatsApp Business Platform.
